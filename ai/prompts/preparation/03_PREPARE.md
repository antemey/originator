# Phase B — prepare the model and independent references

**Owner:** Antoine with Codex in a dedicated operator session, not `impl` or `verify`. This phase may adjust seeds, schemas, contract, adapter and harness before freezing them. It does not implement the engine or its business tests.

Use supplied sanitized observations/captures, active scope and `research/`. Archive the actual prompts and inference/experiment traces under `ai/`. Historical axis notes suggest questions; they do not establish facts.

## 1. Inventory, domain and approach

Inventory existing evidence: established, compatible but not identified, unknown, next discriminating measurement. Record which fields/actions are actually captured and missing. Existing captures are discovery, never retrospectively held-out.

Before interpreting new outcomes, fix the capture domain in `target/context.md`: guest context, country, currency, supported products/variants/quantities, tax/display assumptions and exclusions. Do not claim this was done before already-known observations. Start with the two observed café/accessory references; add another only for a necessary discriminating test.

Do not delete a gift or excluded mechanism to manufacture an admissible basket. If it affects retained amounts, or independence is not established, preserve the capture but do not count it as in-domain fidelity evidence. Distinguish omitted output fields from an omitted mechanism that changes article calculations.

Document 2–4 genuinely different approaches in `WRITEUP.md`, with speed, fidelity, robustness, AI leverage and verification trade-offs. The chosen approach combines a limited Woo-derived data model, source-guided algorithms and target-constrained values. Woo is the open-source reference; Medusa/Saleor are examples in the assignment, not additional mandatory targets.

## 2. Define the limited seed model and record inference

Start the prepared local lab and record versions actually run. Inspect only the native product/coupon/tax/option fields needed for the selected slice, via available local administration tools and pinned source. Do not inventory all Woo settings or infer the merchant's full internal model.

Make `target/seed.schema.json`, runtime validation and `Seed` coherent. Include the supported catalogue, exact price representation/precision, applicable tax rates, coupons and eligibility, and only configuration axes selected for implementation and covered by measured reference cases. Claim support only after clone verification. Map native counterparts in schema descriptions where one exists; identify clone-specific identifiers instead of inventing Woo counterparts.

An unsupported axis has a declared fixed value; reject other supplied values rather than pretending full coverage. No expected totals or basket-specific corrections in the seed. The engine owns algorithms, rounding steps and state transitions. For V1, sequential discounts are fixed to disabled. Record this supported setting explicitly and reject enabled values. Do not prepare or implement any additional discount-combination mode.

Fill `target/seed.json` and a compact field/value/evidence/status table in `target/context.md`. Statuses distinguish directly observed data, inferred explanations and explicit modelling assumptions. Assumptions need not be Woo defaults; identify why each was chosen and remaining alternatives. Café-only coupon eligibility does not identify category restriction rather than product restriction. Displayed TTC does not establish how prices were entered internally. Observed rates apply to the observed references/context, not automatically the entire shop.

## 3. Prepare direct target references and audit the adapter

Specify source-to-projection mappings, currency units, precision and excluded fields. Verify two or three selected projections directly, amount by amount, against sanitized raw responses; include a mixed-rate coupon basket when available. Antoine reviews these checks independently of the adapter. Keep short evidence and adapter tests whose expected values were checked directly, never generated by that adapter.

Convert complete reference responses without monetary reconstruction. Capture action decisions separately; do not invent missing decisions or intermediate states. Reconstructed monetary values remain derived with their formula and source, not direct-target evidence. Incomplete observations stay supporting evidence until completed; do not force them into executable fixtures.

Use a compact coverage set, reusing sequences across needs rather than imposing 8–10 new target captures: nominal coupon eligibility in a mixed basket; recalculation after a quantity/item/coupon change; a measured refusal with unchanged cart; a fresh-session replay. Preserve real messages and outcomes, not presumed ones. Choose one discriminating monetary case for the rounding hypothesis that affects the slice; obtain its reference in the lab or target and label it accordingly. Test residual allocation only if that behaviour is claimed.

Use the fewest target requests needed; record the actual replay and any differences. Each runnable fixture has complete actions/decisions, captured expected projection, provenance and an explicit valid seed association. Contract/reset properties can be tested later without claiming they were observed on Woo's public API.

## 4. Use one lab for the mirror and coupon interaction

Create `target/lab/mirror.json`: café data plus explicit recorded vanilla settings and declared hypotheses. A versioned vanilla starting point is not an unspecified promise that every setting is at its default. Record product-ID correspondence and price basis. Reuse a small number of target recipes and the audited adapter; do not require a full mirrored corpus.

For an equal pair, conclude compatibility with this candidate reference on the tested fields/context. For a difference, investigate data, context, version, settings, extension/hook or projection error; do not automatically label it merchant configuration. Seed differences describe model adjustments on supported axes, not the complete or uniquely identified merchant configuration. Standard mechanism and merchant parameter can coexist in one rule.

Add a second local 20% coupon (`LAB20`) in one dedicated immutable seed under `target/lab/`, derived from the mirror. Configure both coupons as combinable and eligible for the same coffee items; keep the accessory ineligible. Fix sequential discounts to disabled.

Capture one mixed coffee/accessory recipe: add the coffee and accessory, apply `DECOUVERTE10`, apply `LAB20`, then remove `LAB20`. Preserve actual responses for every action and cart snapshots after each of the three coupon steps, for strict comparison of lines, coupons and retained tax fields. Expected values come from actual lab responses, never the clone. Reuse the existing reset/replay checks during implementation.

This single-mode interaction experiment is required for V1 and establishes lab behaviour only, not merchant fidelity. No other discount-combination mode is part of preparation or implementation.

Under time pressure, reduce extra target captures, mirror breadth and optional residual exploration before dropping the retained interaction experiment. If minimum evidence cannot be obtained within the remaining assignment budget, report the missing claim explicitly; do not invent results or silently redefine completion.

## 5. Record rules and pre-register the unseen evaluation

Populate `woo/kernel-model.md` during preparation, not just its header. Initial rule records preserve the inference and experiments; `impl` later adds implementation/source details without rewriting reference evidence or silently changing frozen assumptions.

Header: `reference_source_revision`, `reference_woo_version`, `lab_woo_version`, `target_woo_version`, `target_version_evidence`. Target version may be unknown. Per rule: ID, rule, scope, seed field if relevant, observation, alternatives/hypothesis, discriminating test, evidence by type, verdict and WRITEUP confidence zone. Multiple evidence types may support one rule. Reference pinned source locations; unknown revision stays unknown.

Antoine selects 2–3 new action-only evaluation recipes within the frozen domain before implementation and before their outcomes are collected. Store them outside the repository in `../held-out/recipes/`; publish their hashes and registration date, not their contents, in provenance. Do not expose recipes in role contexts or test-run them to select favourable cases. Their result files do not exist during development. Do not claim OS-level secrecy for operator files merely placed outside Git.

Require: coherent validated seed/schema/contract; at least one complete direct-target fixture and the declared coverage disposition; audited projection mappings; mirror pairs and the V1 non-sequential two-coupon lab experiment; initial rule records; registered recipes; sanitized provenance and actual operator traces. Report any unmet item as unmet, not PASS.

Seal discovery fixtures and seeds with the existing operator command. Run `pnpm check:prep`.
