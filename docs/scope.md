# Agreed scope

Target: L'Atelier des Cafés. Reproduce a narrow article-pricing, coupon eligibility/application, tax and cart-transition slice using a deterministic in-memory TypeScript engine. The observed catalogue and public coupon `DECOUVERTE10` must be established from evidence. Shipping, fees, gifts/BOGO and unsupported mechanisms are excluded. Medusa, Saleor, a general Woo engine and a UI are excluded.

The merchant judges target-fidelity claims. WooCommerce is a separate local laboratory, never a clone runtime dependency. V1 requires two percentage coupons with sequential discounts disabled. This is the only supported combination mode; no other mode is to be prepared or implemented. Multiple-coupon behavior is not demonstrated on the merchant. Lab fixtures explicitly select a separate seed under `target/lab/`.

Preserve `reset(seed)`, `dispatch(action)`, copied `snapshot()` results, both CLI modes, and add/set_qty/remove/apply_coupon/remove_coupon. Quantity zero, invalid actions and unsupported references must be refused without mutation. Each reset discards prior state. Public types remain in `src/engine/contract.ts`.

Compare exactly: line reference/variant/quantity and `line_subtotal`, `line_subtotal_tax`, `line_total`, `line_total_tax`; coupon code/`total_discount`/`total_discount_tax`; totals `total_items`, `total_items_tax`, `total_discount`, `total_discount_tax`; ordered decisions and only actually captured intermediate snapshots. Exclude aggregate `total_tax`, `tax_lines` and `total_price`. Match identities without mistaking display order for calculation order. Missing/extra/duplicate identities fail. Captured messages/codes are compared exactly when expected.

Observable money uses safe-integer minor units. Source precision and intermediate calculations must not be rounded prematurely; document precision, rounding and bounds before implementation. No bigint or decimal library. External JSON starts as `unknown`; missing values never become zero. The adapter validates/projects without recalculation; decisions need action responses, not just a final cart GET. Expectations never come from the clone.

Use the existing `main` repository and compatible installed toolchain/lockfile. No worktrees, CI, dependencies, global settings or agent push. Two fresh sequential role contexts; Antoine selects roles and commits outside them. At most three discovery cycles. Before roles, freeze seeds, schemas/contract, references, adapter, harness and executable controls. Antoine reviews tracked and untracked role changes. Reserved evidence stays outside role access. Final freeze precedes held-out capture; one official evaluation follows. No executable correction after freeze.

Phase A aligns tooling/instructions only. Phase B, when explicitly authorized, inventories evidence, establishes supported settings/catalogue/seeds/scenarios, resolves contract incompatibilities, documents 2–4 candidate approaches in `WRITEUP.md`, and measures the required local lab scenario. Merchant interactions are guest browsing and ephemeral cart actions only. Woo starts only in preparation, on `127.0.0.1`, with explicit versions and no public database.

Phase-B preparation established a configured candidate `target/seed.json`, separate mirror and two-coupon lab seeds, two direct-target discovery fixtures and two lab fixtures. The [target context](../target/context.md) distinguishes observed data from candidate settings; its stub/preparation wording records the historical input checkpoint and is frozen. The [evidence inventory](../ai/traces/preparation/evidence-inventory.md) records coverage, reference limits and Antoine's projection-review decision. Implementation and independent verification are complete. The four discovery scenarios pass, and the original [held-out verdict](../VERDICTS.md) records three PASS outcomes with no differences against the frozen engine. These results establish only the bounded claims in [WRITEUP.md](../WRITEUP.md); prepared references alone do not establish clone fidelity. Coverage limits do not narrow the action or comparison commitments above.

Requested action quantities and returned cart quantities are distinct. The measurement domain includes coffee requests of 1–3 and accessory requests of 1–2; observed coffee cart quantities establish pricing only at 1, while accessory quantities 1 and 2 are measured. A refused coffee request for 3 remains an in-domain refusal case, not evidence of pricing at 3 or a reason to discard that action. See the target context for the exact evidence and contract consequences.

Original discovery evidence is unchanged. The [axes map](notes/axes-map.md) is a hypothesis map, not a coverage requirement. The [constitution](../ai/constitution.md) defines checkpoint rules. One day of actual effort covers the entire assignment, including prior setup.
